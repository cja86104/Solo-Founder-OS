================================================================================
  1. GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

WHAT IS FOUNDERS HELM?
------------------------
Founders Helm is an all-in-one business management platform designed for
founders, freelancers, and small teams. Instead of paying for 10 different
tools, you get everything in one place:

  - CRM              Track contacts, deals, and your sales pipeline.
  - Projects          Manage tasks, track time, and organize work.
  - Content Engine    Create, schedule, and publish content with AI help.
  - Landing Pages     Build web pages to capture leads.
  - Feedback          Embed feedback widgets and collect user input.
  - Automations       Set up workflows that run automatically.
  - Analytics         Track visitors, traffic, and conversions.
  - Command Center    See your SaaS metrics (MRR, churn, etc.).
  - Code Vault        Save and organize code snippets and prompts.
  - AI Advisor        Get AI-powered business advice.


CREATING YOUR ACCOUNT
---------------------
  1. Go to the Founders Helm website and click "Sign Up."
  2. Enter your email address and choose a password.
  3. Click "Create Account."
  4. Check your email inbox for a verification link and click it.
  5. Once verified, you'll be taken to the app where you can create
     your first workspace.


LOGGING IN
----------
  1. Go to the login page.
  2. Enter your email and password.
  3. Click "Sign In."

  TIP: If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password?" on the
  login page. You'll receive a reset link via email.


================================================================================
  2. NAVIGATING THE APP
================================================================================

Once logged in, you'll see a layout with three main areas:


SIDEBAR (LEFT SIDE)
-------------------
The sidebar is your main navigation menu. It's always visible on desktop
and can be opened with the menu icon on mobile. It's organized into
three groups:

  OVERVIEW
    - Dashboard         Your home screen with an overview of all tools.

  PRODUCTS (Your Main Tools)
    - Code Vault        Save and organize code snippets.
    - Landing Pages     Build web pages to capture leads.
    - Feedback          Collect and manage user feedback.
    - Command Center    View SaaS business metrics.
    - CRM               Manage contacts and deals.
    - Content Engine    Create and schedule content.
    - Projects          Manage projects and tasks.
    - Automations       Set up automated workflows.
    - AI Advisor        Chat with an AI business advisor.
    - Analytics         View website traffic and engagement data.

  ACCOUNT
    - Settings          Manage your profile, workspace, billing, and more.

  TIP: You can collapse the sidebar to just icons by clicking the
  "Collapse" button at the bottom. This gives you more screen space.


HEADER (TOP BAR)
----------------
The top bar contains:
  - Breadcrumbs       Shows where you are (e.g., Dashboard > CRM > Deal).
  - Command Menu      Quick search/action bar (press Ctrl+K to open).
  - Theme Toggle      Switch between light and dark mode.
  - Notification Bell See your latest notifications.
  - User Menu         Access profile, team settings, and sign out.


MAIN CONTENT AREA
-----------------
The center of the screen is where all the action happens. Each tool
opens here when you click it in the sidebar.


================================================================================
  3. WORKSPACES & TEAM MEMBERS
================================================================================

WHAT IS A WORKSPACE?
--------------------
A workspace is like a container for one business or project. All your
contacts, deals, content, projects, and automations live inside a
workspace. If you run multiple businesses, you can create separate
workspaces for each one.


CREATING A WORKSPACE
--------------------
  1. Click your workspace name in the sidebar (top area).
  2. Click "Create Workspace" from the dropdown.
  3. Enter a name for your workspace (e.g., "My SaaS Company").
  4. Click Create.


SWITCHING BETWEEN WORKSPACES
-----------------------------
If you have multiple workspaces, click the workspace name in the sidebar
to see a dropdown list. Click any workspace to switch to it. A checkmark
shows which one is currently active.


INVITING TEAM MEMBERS
---------------------
  1. Click your workspace name and select "Manage Members."
  2. Click "Invite Member."
  3. Enter their email address and choose a role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer).
  4. Click Send Invite.


TEAM ROLES EXPLAINED
--------------------
  +----------+------------------------------------------------------------+
  | Role     | What They Can Do                                           |
  +----------+------------------------------------------------------------+
  | Owner    | Full access to everything. Can delete the workspace,       |
  |          | manage billing, and assign roles. One owner per workspace. |
  +----------+------------------------------------------------------------+
  | Admin    | Almost everything the owner can do: manage members, change |
  |          | settings, create/edit/delete all content. Cannot delete    |
  |          | the workspace or change billing.                           |
  +----------+------------------------------------------------------------+
  | Editor   | Can create, edit, and view content, contacts, deals,      |
  |          | projects, automations, and more. Cannot manage team        |
  |          | members, settings, or billing.                             |
  +----------+------------------------------------------------------------+
  | Viewer   | Read-only access. Can see everything but cannot create,   |
  |          | edit, or delete anything.                                  |
  +----------+------------------------------------------------------------+


================================================================================
  4. DASHBOARD (HOME SCREEN)
================================================================================

The Dashboard is the first screen you see when you log in. It gives you
a bird's-eye view of your entire business:

  - Quick Links     Cards for each tool (Code Vault, Landing Pages,
                    Feedback, etc.) with descriptions. Click any card
                    to jump to that tool.

  - Key Metrics     Revenue, active subscribers, growth trends, and
                    activity summaries at a glance.

Think of the Dashboard as your business "home base." From here, you can
quickly get to anything you need.


================================================================================
  5. CODE VAULT
================================================================================

The Code Vault is your personal library for saving code snippets,
AI prompts, and reusable components. Think of it as a recipe book
for code.


SAVING A NEW ITEM
-----------------
  1. Go to Code Vault in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Item" (or the + button).
  3. Give it a title (e.g., "Login Form Component").
  4. Choose the type: Code Snippet, Prompt, Component, or Template.
  5. Select the programming language (e.g., JavaScript, Python).
  6. Paste your code or content into the editor.
  7. Optionally add a description and assign to a collection.
  8. Click Save.


ORGANIZING WITH COLLECTIONS
----------------------------
Collections are like folders. Create collections such as
"React Components," "API Templates," or "Marketing Prompts."

  1. Go to Code Vault > Collections.
  2. Click "New Collection."
  3. Give it a name, pick a color and icon.
  4. Click Create.


FINDING ITEMS
-------------
  - Search            Use the search bar to find items by name.
  - Filter by Type    Show only snippets, prompts, components, or templates.
  - Filter by Language  Show only items in a specific language.
  - Filter by Collection  Show only items in a specific collection.
  - Favorites         Toggle to show only starred items.


================================================================================
  6. LANDING PAGES
================================================================================

Build simple, effective web pages to showcase your product and capture
leads -- without needing a separate website builder.


CREATING A LANDING PAGE
-----------------------
  1. Go to Landing Pages in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Page."
  3. Fill in your page details:
     - Title         The main heading visitors see.
     - Slug          The URL path (e.g., "my-product" becomes /p/my-product).
     - Description   A short summary for search engines.
     - Content       Your page content (hero text, features, etc.).
  4. Click Create.


PUBLISHING YOUR PAGE
--------------------
New pages start as drafts. To make a page public:
  1. Open the page you want to publish.
  2. Change the status to "Published."
  3. Your page is now live at /p/your-slug.


TRACKING PERFORMANCE
--------------------
The Landing Pages dashboard shows you:
  - Total Pages       How many pages you've created.
  - Total Views       How many times your pages have been visited.
  - Total Leads       How many people filled out a form.
  - Conversions       How many visitors took your desired action.


VIEWING LEADS
-------------
Go to Landing Pages > Leads to see everyone who submitted a form on
your landing pages. You'll see their name, email, which page they came
from, and when they submitted.


================================================================================
  7. FEEDBACK
================================================================================

Collect feedback from your customers using embeddable widgets that you
place on your website or app.


CREATING A FEEDBACK WIDGET
---------------------------
  1. Go to Feedback in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Widget."
  3. Configure your widget:
     - Name             Internal name (e.g., "Homepage Feedback").
     - Title            What users see (e.g., "Send us feedback").
     - Theme            Light, dark, or auto.
     - Color            Your brand color for the widget.
     - Position         Where the button appears (bottom-right, etc.).
     - Categories       Types of feedback (bug, feature, question, other).
     - Require Email    Whether users must give their email.
     - Show Emoji Rating  Let users rate with emojis.
  4. Click Create.


EMBEDDING ON YOUR WEBSITE
--------------------------
After creating a widget, you'll get an embed code (HTML/JavaScript).
Copy this code and paste it into your website's HTML, just before the
closing </body> tag. The feedback button will appear automatically.


MANAGING SUBMISSIONS
--------------------
When users submit feedback, it appears in the Submissions tab. You can:
  - Change status     Mark as New, In Review, Resolved, or Archived.
  - Set priority      Low, Medium, High, or Urgent.
  - Add internal notes  Notes only your team can see.
  - Assign to a team member  Route feedback to the right person.
  - Delete            Remove spam (admin/owner only).


FILTERING FEEDBACK
------------------
Use filters at the top to narrow down by:
  - Status            New, In Review, Resolved, Archived.
  - Type/Category     Bug, Feature, Question, Other.
  - Widget            Which widget the feedback came from.
  - Search            Find by content or email.


================================================================================
  8. COMMAND CENTER (SAAS METRICS)
================================================================================

The Command Center is your business intelligence dashboard. It connects
to Stripe to show you real-time SaaS metrics.


OVERVIEW TAB
------------
  - MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)    Monthly income from subscriptions.
  - ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)     MRR x 12.
  - Active Subscribers                 Current paying customers.
  - Churn Rate                         % of customers who cancelled.
  - Growth Rate                        How fast revenue is growing.
  - MRR Chart                          Visual graph of revenue over time.


CUSTOMERS TAB
-------------
  - A table of all customers with name, email, plan, status, and
    lifetime value.
  - At-Risk Customers -- customers who might cancel soon.


REVENUE TAB
-----------
  - Revenue by Plan     How much each plan contributes.
  - Recent Events       Subscriptions, upgrades, downgrades, cancellations.


SYNC TAB
--------
  - Shows when data was last synced from Stripe.
  - You can trigger a manual sync for fresh data.

  NOTE: The Command Center requires a Stripe integration. If you haven't
  connected Stripe, go to Settings > Billing.


================================================================================
  9. CRM (CONTACTS & DEALS)
================================================================================

The CRM helps you manage your relationships and sales pipeline.


CONTACTS
--------
Contacts are people you do business with -- customers, leads, partners.

  Adding a Contact:
    1. Go to CRM (or Contacts) in the sidebar.
    2. Click "New Contact."
    3. Fill in: Name, Email, Phone, Company, Status, Tags.
    4. Click Create.

  Finding Contacts:
    - Search           By name, email, or company.
    - Filter by Status Lead, Active, Inactive, or Churned.
    - Filter by Tags   Show contacts with specific tags.


DEALS (SALES PIPELINE)
-----------------------
Deals represent potential sales. The CRM shows deals as a Kanban board --
columns are stages, and deals are cards you drag between them.

  Default Pipeline Stages:
    Lead          Initial interest.
    Qualified     Confirmed good fit.
    Proposal      Sent a proposal or quote.
    Negotiation   Working out final details.
    Won           Deal closed successfully!
    Lost          Deal didn't work out.

  Creating a Deal:
    1. On the CRM page, click "New Deal."
    2. Fill in: Name, Value ($), Stage, Contact, Notes.
    3. Click Create.

  Moving a Deal:
    Drag and drop the deal card from one column to another, or open the
    deal and change its stage from the dropdown.


CRM STATS
---------
At the top of the CRM page:
  - Total Pipeline Value    Sum of all open deals.
  - Deals Won               How many deals you've closed.
  - Win Rate                 % of deals that ended in "Won."
  - Average Deal Value       Average dollar amount of deals.

  10. CONTENT ENGINE
================================================================================

Create, write, and manage blog posts, social media content, newsletters,
and more -- with built-in AI assistance.


CONTENT TYPES
-------------
  - Blog Post        Long-form articles for your website.
  - Social Post      Short-form content for social media.
  - Newsletter       Email content for subscribers.
  - Video Script     Scripts for video content.

     lists, images, code blocks).
  6. Add tags to categorize your content.

  8. Click Save as Draft or Publish.


CONTENT TABS
------------
  - Posts       All content in list/grid view with status/type/platform filters.
  - Calendar    Visual calendar of scheduled content.
  - Ideas       Capture content ideas for later.
  - Analytics   Content performance (views, likes, shares, comments).


CONTENT STATUSES
----------------
  Draft         Work in progress. Not visible.
  Scheduled     Will auto-publish at a set date/time.

Look for the AI/Sparkles icon when creating or editing content to

  11. PROJECTS & TASKS
================================================================================

Manage your work with projects, tasks, and time tracking.


CREATING A PROJECT
------------------
  1. Go to Projects in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Project."
  3. Enter a name and optional description.
  4. Set start date and due date if applicable.
  5. Click Create.


MANAGING TASKS
--------------
Each project contains tasks -- individual items of work.

  Creating a Task:
    1. Select a project (or "All Tasks").
    2. Click "New Task."
    3. Fill in: Title, Description, Priority (Low/Medium/High/Urgent),
       Status (To Do/In Progress/Review/Done), Due Date, Assignee.
    4. Click Create.

  Task Views:
    - List View     Tasks in a simple list.
    - Board View    Tasks as cards on a Kanban board (columns = statuses).


TIME TRACKING
-------------
Track how much time you spend on tasks:
  1. Open a task or use the Time Tracker panel.
  2. Click "Start Timer" to begin.
  3. Click "Stop" when done.
  4. The time entry is saved and linked to the task.

You can also add time entries manually by entering start/end times.


PROJECT PROGRESS
----------------
Each project shows a progress bar based on the percentage of completed
tasks.

  12. AUTOMATIONS
================================================================================

Create workflows that run automatically when something happens.
Example: "When a new contact is added, send them a welcome email."


HOW AUTOMATIONS WORK
--------------------
Every automation has two parts:
  1. Trigger    The event that starts it (e.g., "new contact created").
  2. Actions    What happens next (e.g., "send email," "create task").


AVAILABLE TRIGGERS
------------------
  Contacts    New contact, Contact updated, Contact tagged
  Deals       Deal created, Deal stage changed, Deal won, Deal lost
  Content     Form submitted, Feedback received
  Projects    Task completed, Task created
  System      Scheduled, Webhook, Manual


AVAILABLE ACTIONS
-----------------
  Send Email       Sends an email to a specified address.
  Create Task      Creates a new task in a project.
  Update Contact   Changes a contact's information.
  Add Tag          Adds a tag to a contact.
  Remove Tag       Removes a tag from a contact.
  Create Deal      Creates a new deal in your pipeline.
  Move Deal        Moves a deal to a different stage.
  Update Deal      Changes a deal's information.
  Webhook          Sends data to an external URL.
  Delay            Waits before continuing.
  Condition        Checks if something is true before continuing.


CREATING AN AUTOMATION
----------------------
  1. Go to Automations in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Automation."
  3. Give it a name and optional description.
  4. Choose a trigger by clicking one of the trigger cards.
  5. Configure the trigger settings.
  6. Add one or more actions by clicking "Add Action."
  7. Configure each action's settings.
  8. Optionally set a daily run limit and cooldown period.
  9. Click "Create Automation."

  NOTE: New automations start as Drafts. Change status to "Active"
  for them to run automatically.


AUTOMATION STATUSES
-------------------
  Draft       Still being set up. Will not run.
  Active      Running. Will trigger when the event occurs.
  Paused      Temporarily stopped. Can be reactivated.
  Archived    Permanently stopped and hidden.


RUNNING MANUALLY
----------------
Test any automation by clicking "Run Now" on its detail page.


VIEWING RUN HISTORY
-------------------
Click "View Runs" on any automation to see every time it ran:
  - Status            Completed, Failed, Running, Pending, or Cancelled.
  - Duration          How long it took.
  - Actions Executed  How many actions ran successfully.
  - Actions Failed    How many had errors.
  - Logs              Step-by-step log of what happened.

================================================================================
  18. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: How do I switch between light and dark mode?
A: Click the sun/moon icon in the top-right corner of the header bar.

Q: Can I use this on my phone?
A: Yes. Founders Helm is fully responsive. On mobile, tap the menu
   icon (three horizontal lines) in the top-left to open the sidebar.

Q: How do I connect Stripe?
A: Go to Settings > Billing. Your Stripe integration is configured
   through environment variables by your administrator. Once connected,
   the Command Center automatically displays your metrics.

Q: Can I have multiple workspaces?
A: Yes. Create as many as you need. Each workspace is completely
   separate -- contacts, deals, content, and everything else is
   isolated per workspace.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Go to Settings > Account, scroll to the bottom, click "Delete
   Account" and confirm. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Q: What happens if I forget my password?
A: On the login page, click "Forgot Password?" Enter your email, and
   you'll receive a reset link.

Q: How do I embed a feedback widget on my website?
A: Go to Feedback, create a widget, copy the embed code, and paste it
   into your website's HTML before the closing </body> tag.

Q: Why is my Command Center empty?
A: It requires Stripe data. If you haven't connected Stripe or don't
   have subscriptions yet, metrics show as zero. Data populates
   automatically once you process payments through Stripe.

Q: How do I make a landing page public?
A: Create a page, change its status to "Published." It will be live
   at /p/your-slug.

Q: Can team members see each other's data?
A: Yes, within the same workspace all members see all data. The role
   system controls what actions they can take (view vs. edit vs.
   delete), not what data they see.

Q: How do automations work with webhooks?
A: When you create a webhook-triggered automation, the system generates
   a unique webhook URL. External services can send data to this URL
   to trigger the automation. HMAC-SHA256 signature verification is
   used for security.
