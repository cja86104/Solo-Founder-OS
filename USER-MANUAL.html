1. Getting Started

What Is Solo Founder OS?

Solo Founder OS is an all-in-one business management platform designed for founders, freelancers, and small teams. Instead of paying for 10 different tools, you get everything in one place:

CRM

Track contacts, deals, and your sales pipeline.

Projects

Manage tasks, track time, and organize work.

Content Engine

Create, schedule, and publish content with AI assistance.

Landing Pages

Build and deploy pages to capture leads.

Feedback

Embed feedback widgets and collect user input.

Automations

Set up workflows that run automatically.

Analytics

Track visitors, traffic, and conversions.

Command Center

See your SaaS metrics (MRR, churn, etc.).

Code Vault

Save and organize code snippets and prompts.

AI Advisor

Get AI-powered business advice.

Creating Your Account

  1. Go to the Solo Founder OS website and click Sign Up.
  2. Enter your email address and choose a password.
  3. Click Create Account.
  4. Check your email inbox for a verification link and click it.
  5. Once verified, you'll be taken to the app where you can create your first workspace.

Logging In

  1. Go to the login page.
  2. Enter your email and password.
  3. Click Sign In.

If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. You'll receive a reset link via email.

2. Navigating the App

Once logged in, you'll see a layout with three main areas:

Sidebar (Left Side)

The sidebar is your main navigation menu. It's always visible on desktop and can be opened with the menu icon on mobile. It's organized into three groups:

Overview

  • Dashboard — Your home screen with an overview of all tools.

Products (Your Main Tools)

  • Code Vault — Save and organize code snippets.
  • Landing Pages — Build web pages to capture leads.
  • Feedback — Collect and manage user feedback.
  • Command Center — View SaaS business metrics.
  • CRM — Manage contacts and deals.
  • Content Engine — Create and schedule content.
  • Projects — Manage projects and tasks.
  • Automations — Set up automated workflows.
  • AI Advisor — Chat with an AI business advisor.
  • Analytics — View website traffic and engagement data.

Account

  • Settings — Manage your profile, workspace, billing, and more.

You can collapse the sidebar to just icons by clicking the "Collapse" button at the bottom. This gives you more screen space.

Header (Top Bar)

The top bar contains:

  • Breadcrumbs — Shows you where you are in the app (e.g., Dashboard > CRM > Deal Details).
  • Command Menu — A quick search/action bar (press Ctrl+K to open).
  • Theme Toggle — Switch between light and dark mode.
  • Notification Bell — See your latest notifications.
  • User Menu — Access your profile, team settings, and sign out.

Main Content Area

The center of the screen is where all the action happens. Each tool opens here when you click it in the sidebar.

3. Workspaces & Team Members

What Is a Workspace?

A workspace is like a container for one business or project. All your contacts, deals, content, projects, and automations live inside a workspace. If you run multiple businesses, you can create separate workspaces for each one.

Creating a Workspace

  1. Click your workspace name in the sidebar (top area).
  2. Click "Create Workspace" from the dropdown.
  3. Enter a name for your workspace (e.g., "My SaaS Company").
  4. Click Create.

Switching Between Workspaces

If you have multiple workspaces, click the workspace name in the sidebar to see a dropdown list of all your workspaces. Click any workspace to switch to it. A checkmark shows which one is currently active.

Inviting Team Members

  1. Click your workspace name and select "Manage Members".
  2. Click "Invite Member".
  3. Enter their email address and choose a role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer).
  4. Click Send Invite.

Team Roles Explained

Role What They Can Do
Owner Full access to everything. Can delete the workspace, manage billing, and assign roles. Only one owner per workspace.
Admin Almost everything the owner can do: manage members, change settings, create/edit/delete all content. Cannot delete the workspace or change billing.
Editor Can create, edit, and view content, contacts, deals, projects, automations, and more. Cannot manage team members, settings, or billing.
Viewer Read-only access. Can see everything but cannot create, edit, or delete anything.

4. Dashboard (Home Screen)

The Dashboard is the first screen you see when you log in. It gives you a bird's-eye view of your entire business:

  • Quick Links — Cards for each tool (Code Vault, Landing Pages, Feedback, etc.) with short descriptions. Click any card to jump to that tool.
  • Key Metrics — Revenue, active subscribers, growth trends, and activity summaries at a glance.

Think of the Dashboard as your business "home base." From here, you can quickly get to anything you need.

5. Code Vault

The Code Vault is your personal library for saving code snippets, AI prompts, and reusable components. Think of it as a recipe book for code.

Saving a New Item

  1. Go to Code Vault in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Item" (or the + button).
  3. Give it a title (e.g., "Login Form Component").
  4. Choose the type: Code Snippet, Prompt, Component, or Template.
  5. Select the programming language (e.g., JavaScript, Python).
  6. Paste your code or content into the editor.
  7. Optionally, add a description and assign it to a collection.
  8. Click Save.

Organizing with Collections

Collections are like folders. You can create collections such as "React Components," "API Templates," or "Marketing Prompts" to keep things organized.

  1. Go to Code Vault > Collections.
  2. Click "New Collection".
  3. Give it a name, pick a color and icon.
  4. Click Create.

Finding Items

  • Search — Use the search bar at the top to find items by name.
  • Filter by Type — Filter to show only snippets, prompts, components, or templates.
  • Filter by Language — Show only items in a specific programming language.
  • Filter by Collection — Show only items in a specific collection.
  • Favorites — Toggle to show only items you've starred as favorites.

6. Landing Pages

Build simple, effective web pages to showcase your product and capture leads — without needing a separate website builder.

Creating a Landing Page

  1. Go to Landing Pages in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Page".
  3. Fill in your page details:
    • Title — The main heading visitors see.
    • Slug — The URL path (e.g., "my-product" becomes yoursite.com/p/my-product).
    • Description — A short summary for search engines.
    • Content — Your page content (hero text, features, etc.).
  4. Click Create.

Publishing Your Page

New pages start as drafts. To make a page visible to the public:

  1. Open the page you want to publish.
  2. Change the status to "Published".
  3. Your page is now live at /p/your-slug.

Tracking Performance

The Landing Pages dashboard shows you:

  • Total Pages — How many pages you've created.
  • Total Views — How many times your pages have been visited.
  • Total Leads — How many people filled out a form on your pages.
  • Conversions — How many visitors took your desired action.

Viewing Leads

Go to Landing Pages > Leads to see everyone who submitted a form on your landing pages. You'll see their name, email, which page they came from, and when they submitted.

7. Feedback

Collect feedback from your customers using embeddable widgets that you place on your website or app.

Creating a Feedback Widget

  1. Go to Feedback in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Widget".
  3. Configure your widget:
    • Name — Internal name for your reference (e.g., "Homepage Feedback").
    • Title — What users see (e.g., "Send us feedback").
    • Theme — Light, dark, or auto (matches the user's device).
    • Color — Your brand color for the widget.
    • Position — Where the widget button appears (bottom-right, bottom-left, etc.).
    • Categories — Types of feedback users can choose (bug, feature, question, other).
    • Require Email — Toggle whether users must provide their email.
    • Show Emoji Rating — Let users rate with emojis.
  4. Click Create.

Embedding on Your Website

After creating a widget, you'll get an embed code (a snippet of HTML/JavaScript). Copy this code and paste it into your website's HTML, just before the closing </body> tag. The feedback button will appear automatically.

Managing Submissions

When users submit feedback through your widget, it appears in the Submissions tab. For each submission, you can:

  • Change status — Mark as New, In Review, Resolved, or Archived.
  • Set priority — Low, Medium, High, or Urgent.
  • Add internal notes — Write notes only your team can see.
  • Assign to a team member — Route feedback to the right person.
  • Delete — Remove spam or irrelevant submissions (admin/owner only).

Filtering Feedback

Use the filters at the top to narrow down submissions by:

  • Status — New, In Review, Resolved, Archived.
  • Type/Category — Bug, Feature, Question, Other.
  • Widget — Which widget the feedback came from.
  • Search — Find feedback by content or email.

8. Command Center (SaaS Metrics)

The Command Center is your business intelligence dashboard. It connects to Stripe to show you real-time SaaS metrics.

What You'll See

The Command Center is organized into tabs:

Overview Tab

  • MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) — How much money you earn every month from subscriptions.
  • ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) — MRR multiplied by 12.
  • Active Subscribers — How many paying customers you have right now.
  • Churn Rate — What percentage of customers cancelled this month.
  • Growth Rate — How fast your revenue is growing.
  • MRR Chart — A visual graph of your revenue over time.

Customers Tab

  • A table of all your customers with their name, email, plan, status, and lifetime value.
  • At-Risk Customers — Customers who might cancel soon (based on behavior patterns).

Revenue Tab

  • Revenue by Plan — See how much each plan contributes.
  • Recent Revenue Events — New subscriptions, upgrades, downgrades, and cancellations.

Sync Tab

  • Shows when data was last synced from Stripe.
  • You can trigger a manual sync if you need fresh data immediately.

The Command Center requires a Stripe integration. Your Stripe data is automatically synced via webhooks. If you haven't connected Stripe yet, go to Settings > Billing to set it up.

9. CRM (Contacts & Deals)

The CRM helps you manage your relationships and sales pipeline. It has two main parts: Contacts and Deals.

Contacts

Contacts are people you do business with — customers, leads, partners, etc.

Adding a Contact

  1. Go to CRM (or Contacts if visible) in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Contact".
  3. Fill in their information:
    • Name — First and last name.
    • Email — Their email address.
    • Phone — Phone number (optional).
    • Company — Where they work (optional).
    • Status — Lead, Active, Inactive, or Churned.
    • Tags — Labels to categorize them (e.g., "VIP," "Enterprise").
  4. Click Create.

Finding Contacts

  • Search — Search by name, email, or company.
  • Filter by Status — Show only leads, active, inactive, or churned contacts.
  • Filter by Tags — Show contacts with specific tags.

Deals (Sales Pipeline)

Deals represent potential sales moving through your pipeline. The CRM shows deals as a Kanban board — columns represent stages, and deals are cards you drag between stages.

Default Pipeline Stages

Stage What It Means
Lead Initial interest — someone who might buy.
Qualified You've confirmed they're a good fit.
Proposal You've sent them a proposal or quote.
Negotiation Working out the final details.
Won Deal closed successfully!
Lost Deal didn't work out.

Creating a Deal

  1. On the CRM page, click "New Deal".
  2. Fill in the details:
    • Name — A name for this deal (e.g., "Acme Corp Annual Plan").
    • Value — How much the deal is worth in dollars.
    • Stage — Where this deal is in your pipeline.
    • Contact — Link it to an existing contact.
    • Notes — Any relevant details.
  3. Click Create.

Moving a Deal

To move a deal to the next stage, simply drag and drop the deal card from one column to another. You can also open the deal and change its stage from the dropdown.

CRM Stats

At the top of the CRM page, you'll see key stats:

  • Total Pipeline Value — The sum of all open deals.
  • Deals Won — How many deals you've closed.
  • Win Rate — The percentage of deals that ended in "Won."
  • Average Deal Value — The average dollar amount of your deals.

10. Content Engine

Create, write, and manage blog posts, social media content, newsletters, and more — all with built-in AI assistance.

Content Types

  • Blog Post — Long-form articles for your website.
  • Social Post — Short-form content for social media.
  • Newsletter — Email content for your subscribers.
  • Video Script — Scripts for video content.
  • Thread — Multi-part social media threads.

Creating Content

  1. Go to Content Engine in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Post".
  3. Choose the content type (blog, social, newsletter, etc.).
  4. Enter a title.
  5. Write your content in the rich text editor, which supports:
    • Bold, italic, headings, lists.
    • Images and media embedding.
    • Code blocks.
  6. Add tags to categorize your content.
  7. Select which platforms to publish to (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn, Blog).
  8. Click Save as Draft or Publish.

Content Tabs

The Content Engine page has several views:

  • Posts — All your content in a list/grid view with filters for status, type, and platform.
  • Calendar — A visual calendar showing when content is scheduled.
  • Ideas — A place to capture content ideas for later.
  • Analytics — See how your content is performing (views, likes, shares, comments).

Content Statuses

Status Meaning
DraftWork in progress. Not visible to anyone.
ScheduledWill be published automatically at a set date/time.
PublishedLive and visible to the public.
ArchivedHidden from public view but kept for records.

AI Content Generation

The Content Engine includes AI assistance to help you write faster. When creating or editing content, look for the AI/Sparkles icon to generate ideas, outlines, or full drafts based on your topic.

11. Projects & Tasks

Manage your work with projects, tasks, and time tracking — perfect for client work, product development, or personal to-dos.

Creating a Project

  1. Go to Projects in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Project".
  3. Enter a name and optional description.
  4. Set a start date and due date if applicable.
  5. Click Create.

Managing Tasks

Each project contains tasks. Tasks are the individual items of work that need to be done.

Creating a Task

  1. Select a project (or choose "All Tasks").
  2. Click "New Task".
  3. Fill in the details:
    • Title — What needs to be done.
    • Description — More details (optional).
    • Priority — Low, Medium, High, or Urgent.
    • Status — To Do, In Progress, Review, or Done.
    • Due Date — When it needs to be finished.
    • Assignee — Who's responsible.
  4. Click Create.

Task Views

  • List View — Tasks in a simple list format.
  • Board View — Tasks as cards on a Kanban board (columns = statuses).

Time Tracking

Track how much time you spend on tasks:

  1. Open a task or use the Time Tracker panel.
  2. Click "Start Timer" to begin tracking.
  3. When you're done, click "Stop".
  4. The time entry is saved automatically and linked to the task.

You can also add time entries manually by entering the start time, end time, and description.

Project Progress

Each project shows a progress bar calculated from the percentage of completed tasks. This gives you a quick overview of how far along each project is.

12. Automations

Automations let you create workflows that run automatically when something happens. For example: "When a new contact is added, send them a welcome email."

How Automations Work

Every automation has two parts:

  1. Trigger — The event that starts the automation (e.g., "new contact created").
  2. Actions — What happens next (e.g., "send email," "create task," "add tag").

Available Triggers

CategoryTriggers
Contacts New contact, Contact updated, Contact tagged
Deals Deal created, Deal stage changed, Deal won, Deal lost
Content Form submitted, Feedback received
Projects Task completed, Task created
System Scheduled (runs on a schedule), Webhook (triggered by external apps), Manual (you start it)

Available Actions

ActionWhat It Does
Send EmailSends an email to a specified address.
Create TaskCreates a new task in a project.
Update ContactChanges a contact's information.
Add TagAdds a tag to a contact.
Remove TagRemoves a tag from a contact.
Create DealCreates a new deal in your pipeline.
Move DealMoves a deal to a different pipeline stage.
Update DealChanges a deal's information.
WebhookSends data to an external URL.
DelayWaits a specified amount of time before continuing.
ConditionChecks if something is true before continuing (e.g., "only if deal value > $1000").

Creating an Automation

  1. Go to Automations in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Automation".
  3. Give it a name and optional description.
  4. Choose a trigger by clicking one of the trigger cards.
  5. Configure the trigger settings (if applicable).
  6. Add one or more actions by clicking "Add Action."
  7. Configure each action's settings.
  8. Optionally set a daily run limit and cooldown period.
  9. Click "Create Automation".

New automations start as Drafts. You must change the status to "Active" for them to run automatically.

Automation Statuses

StatusMeaning
DraftStill being set up. Will not run.
ActiveRunning and will trigger when the event occurs.
PausedTemporarily stopped. Can be reactivated.
ArchivedPermanently stopped and hidden.

Running Manually

You can test any automation by clicking "Run Now" on the automation's detail page. This triggers it immediately with test data.

Viewing Run History

Click "View Runs" on any automation to see a history of every time it ran. For each run, you can see:

  • Status — Completed, Failed, Running, Pending, or Cancelled.
  • Duration — How long it took to run.
  • Actions Executed — How many actions ran successfully.
  • Actions Failed — How many actions encountered errors.
  • Logs — A detailed step-by-step log of what happened.

13. AI Advisor

The AI Advisor is your built-in business consultant. You can chat with it to get advice on strategy, marketing, product decisions, and more.

Starting a Conversation

  1. Go to AI Advisor in the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Conversation".
  3. Choose a topic (Growth, Marketing, Product, Sales, Operations, Finance, or General).
  4. Type your question or describe your situation.
  5. The AI will respond with advice tailored to your business.

Conversation History

All your conversations are saved. You can return to them anytime from the conversation list on the left side. Filter by topic to find specific conversations.

Be specific with your questions for better advice. Instead of "How do I grow?", try "I have a B2B SaaS with 50 customers at $49/month. What are the top 3 strategies to reach $10k MRR?"

18. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Click the sun/moon icon in the top-right corner of the header bar. The app will switch between light and dark themes.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. Solo Founder OS is fully responsive. On mobile, the sidebar is hidden by default — tap the menu icon (three horizontal lines) in the top-left to open it.

How do I connect Stripe?

Go to Settings > Billing. Your Stripe integration is configured through environment variables by your administrator. Once connected, the Command Center will automatically display your SaaS metrics.

Can I have multiple workspaces?

Yes. You can create as many workspaces as you need. Each workspace is completely separate — contacts, deals, content, and everything else is isolated per workspace.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account and scroll to the bottom. Click "Delete Account" and confirm. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

What happens if I forget my password?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password?". Enter your email, and you'll receive a link to reset your password.

How do I embed a feedback widget on my website?

Go to Feedback, create a widget, and copy the embed code. Paste the code into your website's HTML before the closing </body> tag.

Why is my Command Center empty?

The Command Center requires data from Stripe. If you haven't connected Stripe or don't have any subscriptions yet, the metrics will show as zero. Once you start processing payments through Stripe, data will populate automatically.

How do I make a landing page public?

Create a landing page, then change its status to "Published". It will be accessible at /p/your-slug where "your-slug" is the URL slug you chose when creating the page.

Can team members see each other's data?

Yes, within the same workspace, all members can see all data (contacts, deals, content, etc.). The role system controls what actions they can take (view only vs. edit vs. delete), not what data they can see.

How do automations work with webhooks?

When you create a webhook-triggered automation, the system generates a unique webhook URL. External services (like Zapier, Stripe, or your own app) can send data to this URL, which triggers the automation. Each webhook uses HMAC-SHA256 signature verification for security.
